# Stage 1 Output

Analysis and Recommendations:

1. Venue 3 - Lakeview Pavilion:
Capacity: 90 people
Price: $2,500 for a weekend
Vibe: Romantic but very affordable and offers the lowest price point among all venues.
Justification: Suitable for a small or intimate wedding with minimal budget constraints.

2. Venue 4 - Thornfield Manor:
Capacity: 150 people
Price: $18,000
Vibe: Exclusive, corporate, and popular on request
Justification: Offers the highest capacity among all venues but has a longer waitlist due to its reputation.

3. Venue 6 - Sunrise Farm & Vineyard:
Capacity: 130 people
Price: $9,800 for a weekend
Vibe: Natural and trendy with stunning views
Justification: Offers an attractive option for those willing to compromise on capacity but appreciate the natural setting.

4. Venue 7 - The Atrium Club:
Capacity: 180 people
Price: TBD (on request)
Vibe: Flexible and professional
Justification: Offers a wide range of catering options, accommodating both in-house and external caterers.

5. Venues 9 & 14 - Harborside Event Center and Millbrook Inn:
Capacity: 220 people and 160 people respectively
Price: TBD (on request)
Vibe: Modern, elegant, and formal
Justification: Provides a professional environment with catering flexibility and ample parking options.

6. Venues 5 & 7 - The Foundry at Millworks and The Belvedere:
Capacity: 250 people
Price: TBD (on request)
Vibe: Casual, trendy, and very popular on request
Justification: Offers a unique venue with plenty of options for catering and parking.

7. Venues 10 & 8 - Harborside Event Center and Cedar Hollow Retreat:
Capacity: 220 people (Harborside) and 60 people (Cedar Hollow) respectively
Price: TBD (on request)
Vibe: Modern, elegant, and formal for the former, rustic and cozy for the latter

8. Venue 15 - The Warehouse District Loft:
Capacity: 200 people
Price: $8,800 Friday–Sunday
Vibe: Raw, urban and minimalistic

Exclusion Criteria:
- Venue 3 - Lakeview Pavilion due to lack of outdoor backup space or rain tent availability.
- Venues 9 & 14 - Harborside Event Center and Millbrook Inn due to pricing uncertainties (TBD).

Top 3 Recommendations:
1. Venue 6 - Sunrise Farm & Vineyard: Offering a unique, natural setting at an affordable price point.
2. Venue 5 - The Foundry at Millworks: Providing a trendy and flexible environment with catering options.
3. Venue 3 - Lakeview Pavilion: Offering an intimate and cozy atmosphere for an all-inclusive budget.
